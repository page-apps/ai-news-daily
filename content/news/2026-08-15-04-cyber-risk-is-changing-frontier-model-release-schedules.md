## Summary

Anthropic said it currently has no plan to release Model 2 externally, despite using it internally for coding and agentic work. Axios also reported that OpenAI is slowing its upcoming Astra release because it cannot rule out critical cyber capabilities.

## Why it matters

Release timing is becoming a safety control rather than only a product or infrastructure decision. If multiple leading labs adopt this approach, cyber capability evaluations could become a de facto gate for frontier deployment.
