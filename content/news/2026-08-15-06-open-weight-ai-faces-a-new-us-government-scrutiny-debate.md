## Summary

The current US AI framework excludes released open models from its definition of covered frontier models. Axios reported that officials and industry participants are nevertheless discussing pre-release review, national-security evaluation and procurement standards for powerful open-weight systems.

## Why it matters

The policy boundary between open and closed models is becoming harder to defend as capabilities converge. Any review regime could reshape access, release practices and the competitive balance between US and Chinese model developers.
