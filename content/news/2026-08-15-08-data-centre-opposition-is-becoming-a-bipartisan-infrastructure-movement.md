## Summary

Opposition groups are focusing on electricity demand, water use, land use and local economic impacts. Axios reported that Texas shifted from welcoming data centres to imposing a pause while new rules are considered, while Willie Nelson publicly criticised data-centre development.

## Why it matters

AI infrastructure is acquiring the political characteristics of earlier fossil-fuel conflicts, including local opposition and disputes over who pays for shared resources. Permitting, tax incentives and grid access could become material constraints on model deployment.
