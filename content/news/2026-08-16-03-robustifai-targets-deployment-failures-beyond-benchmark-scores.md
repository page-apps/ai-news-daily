## Summary

The workshop takes place at IJCAI-ECAI on 15 August and covers robustness across models, deployment and user interaction. Its agenda includes distribution shift, adversarial inputs, tool use, human interaction, monitoring and assurance.

## Why it matters

Deployment failures increasingly arise from the interaction between models, tools and users rather than from isolated benchmark errors. Treating robustness as a lifecycle property is important for high-impact applications.
