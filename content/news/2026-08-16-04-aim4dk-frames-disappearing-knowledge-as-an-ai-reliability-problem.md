## Summary

The workshop is being held at IJCAI-ECAI on 15 August and focuses on AI modelling for disappearing knowledge. Its stated concerns include data versioning, temporal degradation, reproducibility and the loss of sources that support model outputs.

## Why it matters

AI systems increasingly depend on information that changes, disappears or becomes difficult to verify. Preserving provenance and measuring drift will be essential for dependable long-lived systems.
