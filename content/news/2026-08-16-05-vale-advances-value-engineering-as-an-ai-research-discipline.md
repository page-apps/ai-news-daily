## Summary

VALE 2026 is being held on 15 August at IJCAI-ECAI and accepted 16 contributions from a record number of submissions. Its topics include value learning, conflict resolution, value-driven explainability, participatory design and legal questions.

## Why it matters

As AI systems make more consequential choices, technical performance alone is not enough to determine acceptable behaviour. Value representation and conflict resolution are becoming concrete engineering problems.
