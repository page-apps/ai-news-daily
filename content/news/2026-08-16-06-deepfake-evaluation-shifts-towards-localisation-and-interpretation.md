## Summary

The second Deepfake Detection, Localization and Interpretability Challenge uses a multimodal deepfake description dataset enriched with Qwen3-VL. It evaluates more than image-level classification by targeting localisation and interpretable traces of manipulation.

## Why it matters

Detection systems that only classify content as real or fake provide limited help for investigators and platforms. Localisation and interpretability are more useful for verification, remediation and accountability.
