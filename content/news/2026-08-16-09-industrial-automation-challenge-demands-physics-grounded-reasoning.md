## Summary

The Industrial Automation Challenge requires models to use structured industrial context, including Failure Mode and Effects Analysis, to produce causal maintenance narratives. It includes both native-model reasoning and agentic tool-augmented tracks.

## Why it matters

Industrial deployment requires decisions that are grounded in physical systems rather than plausible text alone. Evaluating causal justification and tool use is a more realistic test of operational AI than generic language benchmarks.
