## Summary

The challenge evaluates multi-day travel plans against spatial, temporal and financial constraints using tools such as flights and hotels. Its second track tests specialised tool-use plugins for commercial agent platforms, with final results scheduled for presentation at IJCAI-ECAI.

## Why it matters

Planning tasks expose failures that simple question answering misses, especially when agents must satisfy several constraints at once. Travel is a visible test case for the broader transition from conversational systems to tool-using operators.
