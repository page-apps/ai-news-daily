## Summary

An Axios analysis found that Alphabet and Amazon's return on invested capital had risen, Microsoft's was broadly steady and Meta's had declined. It cautioned that off-balance-sheet obligations may make reported returns look more favourable than underlying economics.

## Why it matters

The results weaken claims that AI investment is already destroying hyperscaler economics, but they do not establish that the spending will earn adequate long-term returns.
