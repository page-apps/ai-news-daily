## Summary

Qwen’s model card describes native 262,144-token context, extensibility to 1 million tokens, tool-use support and compatibility with Transformers, vLLM and SGLang. It reports scores of 61.7 on SWE-bench Pro, 73.0 on Terminal Bench 2.1 and 79.0 on QwenSWEBench; these are vendor-reported results awaiting independent validation.

## Why it matters

A capable 27B model that can run on comparatively accessible hardware broadens the practical options for private, local coding agents.
