## Summary

Researchers told TechCrunch that frontier models often refuse exploitability analysis, even when the work is intended to validate and patch vulnerabilities. Some are consequently turning to locally run open models with fewer restrictions.

## Why it matters

This exposes a central dual-use policy problem: overly broad safeguards may push legitimate defenders towards less accountable systems while doing little to prevent determined attackers.
