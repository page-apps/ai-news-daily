## Summary

Claude voice mode can now use Opus, Sonnet or Haiku, with the default based on the model last used in text chat. Anthropic also says voice conversations can interact with services including Gmail, Google Calendar, Slack, Canva and Notion.

## Why it matters

The change moves voice assistants closer to operational agents that can complete tasks across business software, while also increasing the consequences of permission and reliability failures.
