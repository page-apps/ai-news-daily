## Summary

Health in ChatGPT can connect supported medical records and Apple Health data to provide answers grounded in a user’s personal context. OpenAI says connected health information and related conversations are not used to train its foundation models, and the product is not intended for diagnosis or treatment.

## Why it matters

The rollout puts a general-purpose chatbot directly into a high-stakes domain where privacy, medical reliability and user over-reliance are difficult to separate.
