## Summary

OpenAI said it will isolate training workloads and networks more aggressively, run continuous security tests and monitor model actions, chain-of-thought and logs. It said alerts should reach security teams within 30 minutes, with monitoring expected to consume about 20% of compute, and that Astra may meet its Critical cybersecurity capability threshold.

## Why it matters

Cyber capability is becoming an operational control on frontier-model development and release.
