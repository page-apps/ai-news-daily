## Summary

Synthefy said its Structured Data Foundation Models are designed for numbers, tables, transactions and signals rather than language, and announced the 30-million-parameter Nori-30M model. The company reported that Nori-30M-thinking surpassed a larger Google TabFM model on public regression benchmarks and that Wing Venture Capital led a $6.5 million seed round.

## Why it matters

The launch points to a parallel foundation-model track for structured scientific, industrial and economic data rather than general-purpose language.
