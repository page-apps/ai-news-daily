## Summary

ValidateHTML found that about 87.2% of the domains had invalid HTML, alongside 100,305 HTML violations and 18,863 CSS errors. More than one-third failed accessibility checks, including missing alternative text and ARIA labels.

## Why it matters

The results show that basic standards compliance remains a major operational gap for web teams despite mature tooling and established accessibility guidance.
