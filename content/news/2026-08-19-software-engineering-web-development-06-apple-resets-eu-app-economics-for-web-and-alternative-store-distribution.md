## Summary

The revised terms introduce a flat 5% commission on digital goods sold through apps distributed outside the App Store or through the web. Apple also set standard rates of 26% for in-app purchases and 20% for alternative payments, while loosening financial qualification rules for alternative marketplaces.

## Why it matters

Web checkout and alternative distribution become more commercially viable for EU developers, though teams must account for program-specific exceptions and the terms’ lock-in conditions.
