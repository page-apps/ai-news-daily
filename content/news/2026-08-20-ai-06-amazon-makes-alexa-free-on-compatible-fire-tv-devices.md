## Summary

Amazon is making Alexa+ available at no extra charge on compatible Fire TV devices in the United States, including for customers without Prime, TechCrunch reported. The assistant supports conversational content search, smart-home controls and AI recommendations, while the previously announced non-Prime monthly charge no longer applies to this Fire TV access.

## Why it matters

Putting a more capable conversational assistant into a mass-market television platform gives Amazon a large consumer distribution channel for ambient, voice-driven AI.
