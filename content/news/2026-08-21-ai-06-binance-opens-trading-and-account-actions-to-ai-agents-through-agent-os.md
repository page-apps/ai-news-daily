## Summary

The platform supports compatible agents through MCP, with user-configured permissions, dedicated subaccounts and revocable access; the initial implementation can read market and account data and place trades. Binance says it monitors resulting orders, but the selected AI application's broader workflow and reasoning remain outside Binance's visibility.

## Why it matters

It makes financial actions a first-class agent capability while exposing the limits of exchange-side oversight and the need for stronger permissioning and auditability.
