## Summary

GSE plans to integrate Nuclearn capabilities for natural-language scenario creation, automated performance summaries, adaptive quizzes and plant-specific physics explanations. Nuclearn also plans simulator-informed risk analysis, engineering-modification sandboxes, drift detection and support for evaluating new reactor designs; the announcement describes planned capabilities, not completed deployment.

## Why it matters

Nuclear operations are a high-consequence test of grounded AI, where simulator data and traceable procedures provide a safer path to assistance than unsupervised live control.
