## Summary

TIER IV plans to optimise Autoware and its end-to-end models on R-Car Gen 5, targeting solutions from Level 2+/2++ driver assistance to Level 4 autonomy. The companies plan a demonstration on R-Car X5H at Automotive World 2026; the announcement is a collaboration roadmap rather than a production deployment.

The partnership connects an open autonomous-driving software ecosystem with automotive-grade AI compute and could lower barriers to deploying continuously updated vehicle intelligence.
