## Summary

Starcloud announced a $250 million Series A extension at a $2.3 billion post-money valuation, taking total capital raised to $450 million. It said the funding will support Starcloud-3 manufacturing and work with NVIDIA on a Space-1 Vera Rubin module, while its proposed 88,000-satellite and 20-gigawatt constellation remains a roadmap.

## Why it matters

The round ties major compute and networking investors to a speculative response to AI's energy and cooling constraints.
