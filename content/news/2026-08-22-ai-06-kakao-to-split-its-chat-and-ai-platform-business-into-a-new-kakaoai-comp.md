## Summary

Kakao said it will spin out the KakaoTalk-centred platform business into a new company tentatively named KakaoAI, while KakaoX retains investment operations. Reuters reports that KakaoAI will cover AI, advertising, commerce and KakaoTalk, with a planned January 2027 relisting and a 2030 revenue target of at least 6 trillion won.

## Why it matters

The restructuring shows a major consumer platform reorganising capital and governance around AI rather than treating it as a feature inside a broader conglomerate.
