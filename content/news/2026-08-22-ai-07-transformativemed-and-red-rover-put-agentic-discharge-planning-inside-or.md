## Summary

TransformativeMed said its Agentic Clinical Operating System will combine with Red Rover Health's interoperability layer and Model Context Protocol connections to access clinical context and workflow tools. Their first joint project is agentic patient-discharge planning with an academic health system, with a demonstration planned for the Oracle Health Summit in September.

## Why it matters

Embedding agents in the EHR shifts clinical AI from a separate assistant to an action layer inside operational workflows, where permissions and oversight become central.
