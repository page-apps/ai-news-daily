## Summary

The AVIEW platform will analyse chest CT studies for lung-cancer screening and return structured findings inside INFINITT's PACS, including nodule assessment and prior-image comparison. The companies described it as their second North American deployment and said the repeatable integration model is intended to expand to more hospitals.

## Why it matters

Routine PACS integration is a stronger adoption signal than a standalone pilot because it places AI inside the workflow radiologists already use.
