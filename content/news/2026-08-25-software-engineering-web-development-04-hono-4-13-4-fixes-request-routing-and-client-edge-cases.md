## Summary

Hono 4.13.4 fixes request-parameter handling on unmatched requests and wildcard middleware association in the regexp router. It also corrects JSX ref cleanup, ETag parsing, undefined header and cookie values, form-data cloning, client query serialisation and media-type matching.

## Why it matters
