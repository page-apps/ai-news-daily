## Summary

Version 151 is the final four-week release, with version 152 planned for late August and version 153 planned for the week of 10 September. The SDK remains on a fixed monthly cadence and ships as needed, while evergreen apps update automatically and fixed-version apps require more frequent testing and updates.

## Why it matters

Teams embedding WebView2 need shorter compatibility-testing and rollout loops, particularly when they pin a browser runtime instead of using evergreen updates.
