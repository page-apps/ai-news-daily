## Summary

The release demonstrates state exchange across Windows, macOS and Linux, with local developer diagnostics for inspecting exchanged AI state. Intersignal says the current system works over LAN or Wi-Fi and that managed NAT traversal is planned, while the core experience remains free.

## Why it matters

Local state portability could make multi-machine agent workflows easier to audit and less dependent on a central service, although production durability and interoperability remain unproven.
