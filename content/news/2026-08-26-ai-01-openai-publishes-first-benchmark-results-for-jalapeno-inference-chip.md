## Summary

OpenAI says Jalapeño delivered higher throughput, lower latency and better energy efficiency than comparison systems when serving GPT-OSS 120B, DeepSeek R1 and Kimi K2.5 1T. The results are vendor benchmarks for inference hardware, not training, and OpenAI says limited systems will be deployed this year.

## Why it matters

The results show frontier-model providers are increasingly designing specialised inference hardware to control cost, capacity and latency, although OpenAI remains dependent on Nvidia for training.
