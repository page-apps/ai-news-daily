## Summary

Jetson Orin Nano 2 provides 78 TOPS of AI compute, 8GB of memory and an eight-core CPU, with Nvidia claiming twice the compute and 40% lower power use than its predecessor in a 15-watt mode. The module and developer kit are scheduled for general availability in the first half of 2027, with drone and industrial-robotics partners already exploring deployments.

## Why it matters

More capable and efficient edge hardware lowers the latency and connectivity requirements for autonomous robots, drones and other systems that must respond in the physical world.
