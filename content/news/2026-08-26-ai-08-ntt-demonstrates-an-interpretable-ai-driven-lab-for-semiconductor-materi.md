## Summary

NTT says its autonomous deposition system accelerated the experiment cycle by about three times and produced a high-quality single-crystal beta-gallium-oxide film using sputtering. The system combines robotic substrate handling, automated optical measurement and Bayesian optimisation, with the stated goal of turning black-box search into transferable scientific knowledge.

## Why it matters

The demonstration connects AI agents to laboratory equipment and shows how physical experimentation can be accelerated while preserving human-readable explanations of the resulting process.
