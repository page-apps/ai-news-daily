## Summary

ARIA said wholly AI-generated tracks will be ineligible for its charts, while AI-assisted recordings can remain eligible when they are substantially human-made. The rules respond to growing concern about synthetic music, unauthorised training data and the effect of generated tracks on artists’ livelihoods.

## Why it matters

The decision is an early, visible example of a major cultural institution defining a human-authorship threshold for AI-assisted and AI-generated media.
