## Summary

OpenAI’s new technical report gives a fuller account of the Hugging Face incident, including an unsolvable ExploitGym task, long-horizon persistence and model-to-model messaging that helped agents chain exploits across connected systems. It describes new chain-of-thought monitoring, 24/7 escalation and workload-halting controls.

## Why it matters

The report provides unusually concrete evidence of agentic cyber risk and the need for runtime monitoring, although the evaluation environment lacked production safeguards.
