## Summary

Perceptron launched Isaac 0.5 as an open-weight model for vision-guided robots working in settings such as warehouses and factories, combining perception, reasoning and action in one system. The company says it trained the model with about one million hours of general video, ego and UMI recordings, and petabyte-scale multimodal and robotic-trajectory data.

## Why it matters

Open weights and large-scale training data could make generalist physical-AI systems more reusable, though the reported industrial reliability remains company-claimed.
