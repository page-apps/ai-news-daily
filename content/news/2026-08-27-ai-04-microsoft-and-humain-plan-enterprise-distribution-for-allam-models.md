## Summary

HUMAIN and Microsoft announced a long-term collaboration centred on making HUMAIN’s ALLAM Arabic-language models available through Microsoft Foundry and the Microsoft 365 Copilot ecosystem. The companies also plan to pair HUMAIN specialists with Microsoft’s forward-deployed engineers to help organisations move from pilots to production across Saudi Arabia and the wider region.

## Why it matters

The agreement links a sovereign, local-language model provider with a global enterprise distribution platform, although it remains an announced plan rather than proof of deployment.
