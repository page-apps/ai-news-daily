## Summary

MiniMax reported first-half revenue of US$116.6 million, up 283.1% year on year, while revenue from its Open Platform and other AI enterprise services rose 703.1% to US$73.9 million. Gross profit increased sharply, but adjusted net loss reached US$293.0 million, showing that commercial scale and compute-heavy spending are expanding together.

## Why it matters

The results offer a rare current disclosure of Chinese AI commercialisation and economics, while showing that strong usage growth has not yet translated into profitability.
