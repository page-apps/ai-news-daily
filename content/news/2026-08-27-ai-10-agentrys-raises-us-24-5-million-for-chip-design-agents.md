## Summary

Agentrys announced US$24.5 million across a US$19.1 million seed round led by Etna Labs and a US$5.4 million pre-seed led by MediaTek. The company says its agentic design platform has taken a 32-bit CPU from specification to sign-off-clean GDS layout without a human in the loop and exceeded 90% on NVIDIA’s CVDP benchmark; those are company claims, not independent validation.

## Why it matters

Semiconductor design is a high-value, objectively testable domain where agentic automation could affect AI hardware supply, although the company and demonstrations are early-stage.
