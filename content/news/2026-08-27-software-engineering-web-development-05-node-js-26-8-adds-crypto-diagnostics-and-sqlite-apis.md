## Summary

Node.js 26.8 enables SIV and GCM-SIV modes in Cipher and Decipher, marks TracingChannel stable, improves histogram and net.BlockList behaviour, and adds non-throwing MIMEType.parse. It also adds SQLite StatementSync close and dispose methods, basic REPL syntax highlighting and ZipFile, ZipEntry and ZipBuffer support, alongside certificate and Web Crypto fixes.

## Why it matters

These changes affect a large base of JavaScript services and make security, observability and embedded-data workflows easier to build without external packages.
