## Summary

The @ai-sdk/zai 2.0.0 release adds a Z.AI provider with GLM chat completions, streaming, reasoning, tool use and multimodal inputs. It also backports GLM-5.3-Flash model support to the Z.AI provider and AI Gateway.

## Why it matters

Web developers can route Z.AI models through the same Vercel AI SDK abstractions used for other providers, reducing bespoke integration work while widening model choice.
