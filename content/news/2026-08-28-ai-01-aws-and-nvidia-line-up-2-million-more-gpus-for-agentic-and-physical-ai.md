## Summary

Amazon and NVIDIA announced an expanded partnership adding another 2 million NVIDIA GPUs to AWS data centres in 2027 and 2028, including Blackwell Ultra, Rubin and Rubin Ultra. The agreement also extends NVIDIA networking, CPUs, open models and physical-AI systems across AWS, with no financial terms disclosed.

The commitment shows AI demand continuing to translate into hyperscale compute procurement while linking cloud, model and robotics infrastructure more tightly.
