## Summary

Companies including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, CrowdStrike and Okta signed an open letter calling for public-private cooperation and new defences against AI-enabled cyberattacks. The letter urges collaboration at local, national and international levels and warns that critical infrastructure could be targeted.

## Why it matters

The joint statement shows major AI providers and security firms treating AI-enabled cyber risk as a shared infrastructure problem rather than an isolated model-safety issue.
