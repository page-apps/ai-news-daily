## Summary

Anthropic released the Model Hardware Standard, a model-agnostic framework for connecting AI agents to equipment including microscopes, manufacturing machines, quantum hardware and robot arms. The framework builds on MCP and is being shared with partners while Anthropic develops safety evaluations ahead of a planned open-source release.

## Why it matters

A common interface for physical systems could accelerate scientific and industrial automation, while also making hardware-level safety controls a central part of agent deployment.
