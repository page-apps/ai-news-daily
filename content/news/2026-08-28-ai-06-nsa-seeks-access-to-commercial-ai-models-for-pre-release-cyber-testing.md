## Summary

NSA deputy director Tim Kosiba said the agency wants access to all commercial AI models and is discussing arrangements with leading developers. Under a voluntary White House pre-release testing framework, the NSA could receive up to 30 days of access for classified cyber-capability tests, although no operational programme or participating labs were confirmed.

## Why it matters

If implemented, intelligence-agency access could become a new pre-release control point for frontier models with significant implications for secrecy, oversight and national security.
