## Summary

Bowen told Guardian Australia that new datacentre standards will require 100% renewables backed by firming, while state-owned power providers may apply to prove that non-renewable supply is cheaper. The federal government expects the rules to be debated in parliament next year as electricity demand from datacentres is forecast to rise sharply.

## Why it matters

Australia is moving from voluntary discussion towards national energy conditions that could directly constrain the location and economics of AI datacentre expansion.
