## Summary

ABot-Recon uses a fixed local context window and incremental composition rather than maintaining long-range memory anchors. Amap reports 24.45 frames per second on KITTI-02, lower trajectory error on Oxford Spires, and peak memory use of about 6.71GB.

## Why it matters

Efficient spatial reconstruction is a foundational capability for autonomous driving, embodied-agent training and robots operating without pre-built maps.
