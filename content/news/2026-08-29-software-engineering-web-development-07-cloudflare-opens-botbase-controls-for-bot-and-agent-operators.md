## Summary

The update adds a dashboard home with a bot directory, submission history, status explanations, and controls to edit or cancel submissions. It also expands classification around behaviour, content use, and identity while leaving site owners in control of how traffic is handled.

## Why it matters

Web operators and agent builders gain an explicit governance surface for automated traffic instead of relying only on user-agent conventions and ad hoc blocking.
