## Summary

The release adds a WebMCP layer to Spire storefronts for reading session and cart context, searching products with account-specific pricing, and adding, updating, or removing cart lines. It also improves server-side rendering resource use and adds storefront error timestamps and reference IDs for faster troubleshooting.

## Why it matters

Commerce developers can expose controlled, structured actions to browser agents while preserving existing pricing, promotion, and entitlement logic.
