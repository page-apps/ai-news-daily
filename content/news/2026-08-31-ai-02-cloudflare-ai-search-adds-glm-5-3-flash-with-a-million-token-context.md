## Summary

Cloudflare's 30 August changelog says AI Search can now use GLM-5.3 Flash for text generation through Workers AI. The entry lists a 1,048,576-token context window, but it is a managed deployment announcement rather than an independent evaluation.

## Why it matters

This puts a very long-context open-weight model inside a managed retrieval and search workflow, giving builders a new deployment option. Its production value will depend on latency, cost and quality, none of which the changelog quantifies.
