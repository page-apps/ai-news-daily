## Summary

Anthropic said standard weekly limits for Pro, Max, Team and seat-based Enterprise plans will permanently rise 25% from 14 September, while the current 50% boost remains until then. A follow-up quantified the change against the active promotion as a 17% reduction in weekly capacity, making this a meaningful usage-policy change rather than a straightforward increase.

The distinction between baseline and promotional capacity changes how paid users assess model access, cost and provider dependence. It also shows how rapidly growing agent workloads are feeding directly into subscription policy.
