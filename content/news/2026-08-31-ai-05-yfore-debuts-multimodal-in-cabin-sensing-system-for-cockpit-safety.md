## Summary

YFORE debuted an occupant-and-driver monitoring system combining an automotive-grade smart-mirror camera with cabin radar. The company says it covers fatigue, distraction, unresponsive-driver, phone-use and occupant-classification detection, along with life-presence monitoring; the claims have not been independently validated.

## Why it matters

The system illustrates physical AI moving beyond camera-only driver monitoring towards multimodal sensing of vehicle occupants and hidden physiological signals. Deployment significance will depend on validation, false-positive rates and regulatory acceptance.
