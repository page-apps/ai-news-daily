## Summary

Lesotho's Ministry of Agriculture, Food Security and Nutrition launched a national portal consolidating farmer, land, household, livestock and support-service records across all 10 districts. The system uses Joget DX Enterprise, whose supplier describes its agentic-AI platform and LLM-assisted development process as future application headroom rather than claiming that autonomous AI currently makes agricultural decisions.

## Why it matters

It is a concrete public-sector deployment of an AI-capable application platform in a national service, showing how smaller administrations may use agentic tooling to build systems with limited teams. The evidence is supplier-led and does not independently measure the AI contribution.
