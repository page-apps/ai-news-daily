## Summary

Next.js 16.4.0-canary.12 stores SST keys in key order when hashes are omitted and honours non-interactive mode in upgrade codemod prompts. The release also extends codemod transformation to .mjs files and removes an unnecessary CI workflow.

## Why it matters

More predictable codemods and configuration output lower upgrade risk for large Next.js applications and automated build systems.
