## Summary

The Commission designated ChatGPT as a Very Large Online Search Engine after OpenAI reported more than 45 million average monthly EU users. The designation brings additional systemic-risk, auditing and data-access obligations.

## Why it matters

It extends large-platform regulation to a generative AI service and creates a precedent for supervising chatbots as information intermediaries.
