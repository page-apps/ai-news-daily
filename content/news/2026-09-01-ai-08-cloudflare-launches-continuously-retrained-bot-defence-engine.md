## Summary

Adaptive Intelligence is built into Cloudflare Bot Management and uses network-wide traffic signals to update its machine-learning model continuously. The system is designed to deploy disposable rules, validate new versions in shadow mode and adapt to new bypass tools without scheduled releases.

## Why it matters

It represents a shift towards continuously changing defensive systems as AI and cheap automation reduce the cost of adapting attacks.
