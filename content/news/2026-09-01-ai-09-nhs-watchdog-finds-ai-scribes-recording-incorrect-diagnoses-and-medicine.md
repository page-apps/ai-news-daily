## Summary

Patients identified cases where AI scribes confused diagnoses, substituted similarly named medicines or omitted prescription instructions that clinicians had missed. Twenty-seven AI scribes are in use across England, while the MHRA does not classify the tools as medical devices for nationwide oversight.

## Why it matters

The findings expose the gap between rapid clinical deployment and the governance, review processes and accountability needed for generated medical records.
