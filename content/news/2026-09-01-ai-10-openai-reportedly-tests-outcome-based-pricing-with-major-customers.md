## Summary

The Information reported that some large OpenAI customers can pay only when the system completes defined work, initially including customer-support interactions. The arrangement is selective, has not been publicly announced by OpenAI and has not been independently verified by The Next Web.

## Why it matters

Charging for completed outcomes would shift failure risk from customers to the model provider and challenge token- and seat-based enterprise pricing.
