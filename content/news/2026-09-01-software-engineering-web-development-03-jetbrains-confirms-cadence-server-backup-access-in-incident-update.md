## Summary

JetBrains said Cadence, a hosted service for running PyCharm projects on cloud compute, was accessed through a compromised environment. The August 31 update confirmed access to a 2024 Cadence server backup while stating that no additional compromised resources or data had yet been identified.

## Why it matters

Hosted development and execution services can expose source, artefacts, logs and credentials beyond the IDE itself, making cloud-side execution boundaries a software-supply-chain concern.
