## Summary

The platform combines VMware Cloud Foundation with heterogeneous accelerator support, NVMe memory tiering, storage deduplication, token monitoring, multi-tenant model sharing and GPU observability. Broadcom also says customers can access more than 150 validated open and commercial models through integrated deployment and operations services.

## Why it matters

It packages model serving, infrastructure lifecycle, cost controls and observability into a private deployment path for organisations keeping data and models under local control.
