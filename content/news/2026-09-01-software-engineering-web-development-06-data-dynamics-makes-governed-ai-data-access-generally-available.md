## Summary

Enterprise 2.0.5 adds bring-your-own-model deployment inside an organisation’s trust boundary and content-aware, time-bound entitlements for people, models and agents. It also provides attestable compliance evidence and data-lifecycle controls across cloud, on-premises, sovereign and air-gapped deployments.

## Why it matters

Putting expiring access and audit evidence at the data layer can reduce the need to recreate governance separately in every agent or application.
