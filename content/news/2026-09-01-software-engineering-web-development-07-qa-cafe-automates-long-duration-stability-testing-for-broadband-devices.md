## Summary

The new testing workflow repeatedly exercises broadband and Wi-Fi devices while monitoring CPU and memory through TR-181 over TR-069 or USP. It correlates resource trends with pass/fail results, logs and packet captures to expose leaks, exhaustion, crashes and gradual performance degradation.

## Why it matters

It turns long-horizon reliability checks into repeatable regression work that can run overnight or over a weekend before deployment.
