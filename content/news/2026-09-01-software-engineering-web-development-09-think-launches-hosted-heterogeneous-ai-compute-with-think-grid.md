## Summary

Each initial SuperNode combines four NVIDIA PRO 6000 Blackwell accelerators with 384GB of pooled VRAM, networking, storage, cooling and support. The service is available to Saudi government and enterprise customers from Riyadh and is being rolled out globally for inference, training and agent workloads.

## Why it matters

A managed, heterogeneous compute service gives AI application teams another deployment route between owning infrastructure and renting fragmented hyperscaler capacity.
