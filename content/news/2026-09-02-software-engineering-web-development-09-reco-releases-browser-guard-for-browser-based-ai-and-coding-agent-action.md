## Summary

Browser Guard inspects prompts and agent activity, correlating them with user, data, destination and business context. Reco said the product can detect, guide, block or stop activity involving AI assistants, copilots, coding agents and autonomous workflows.

## Why it matters

The product targets a growing blind spot where browser-based agents can access business data and trigger actions outside conventional application-security controls.
