## Summary

Anthropic released Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1 as the same underlying model with different safeguards: Fable is generally available, while Mythos remains restricted to trusted cybersecurity and life-sciences programmes. Anthropic says cache-read pricing makes typical Fable workloads about 25% cheaper and highly agentic workloads up to 45% cheaper, while new safeguards cut cybersecurity false-positive blocks by 60%; customer-controlled Enterprise Frontier Safeguards are planned for later this year.

## Why it matters

The release shows frontier labs competing on capability, operating cost and calibrated access controls at the same time, rather than treating safety restrictions as a separate product layer.
