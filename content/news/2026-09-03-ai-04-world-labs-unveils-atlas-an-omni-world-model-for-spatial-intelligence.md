## Summary

World Labs introduced Atlas as an omni model pretrained from scratch to operate on text, images, video and 3D through shared spatial context. The company demonstrates camera-controlled generation up to one minute at 1440p, reconstruction from one to dozens of images and space-time simulation for robotics; access is initially limited to selected partners.

## Why it matters

Atlas connects generative media with spatial reasoning and real-to-sim workflows, making world models a plausible foundation for future physical-AI systems while leaving performance claims to be independently tested.
