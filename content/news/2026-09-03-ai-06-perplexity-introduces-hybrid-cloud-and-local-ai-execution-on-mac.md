## Summary

Perplexity introduced Hybrid Compute in its Mac app, splitting Computer tasks between cloud models for reasoning, search and planning and local models for sensitive files and on-device actions. Its privacy gate can mask information, keep it local, refuse the action or request consent before data leaves the device; the feature requires an Apple silicon Mac with at least 24GB of unified memory.

## Why it matters

The design offers a practical compromise between frontier-model capability and data residency, but the privacy outcome still depends on the classifier, policy rules and user permissions.
