## Summary

CrowdStrike introduced SafeMind, a Falcon-native agentic system with Red Tempest offensive and Blue Solano defensive models built using NVIDIA Nemotron and CrowdStrike telemetry. CrowdStrike reports 29% higher detection, six-times-faster remediation and 99% lower cost against leading frontier and open models, but those figures are company evaluations without disclosed independent validation.

## Why it matters

SafeMind signals a shift from AI assistants that identify threats towards specialised systems that can continuously simulate attacks and take defensive action.
