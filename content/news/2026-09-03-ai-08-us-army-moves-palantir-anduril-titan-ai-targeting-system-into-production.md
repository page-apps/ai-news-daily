## Summary

The US Army moved its Tactical Intelligence Targeting Access Node programme into production through delivery orders worth $127 million to Palantir and $65 million to Anduril for eight systems. TITAN combines space, aerial and terrestrial sensor data into actionable targeting information, while Palantir supplies the software and Anduril handles hardware and shelter integration.

## Why it matters

The award turns AI-assisted battlefield sensing and targeting from a prototype effort into an operational procurement programme, increasing the importance of reliability, human control and accountability.
