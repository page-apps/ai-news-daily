## Summary

iPronics announced a $125 million Series B co-led by Maverick Silicon and Light Street Capital, with NVIDIA participating, bringing its total funding to $177 million. The company plans to scale its ONE optical networking engine, which combines programmable switching, telemetry and APIs to reconfigure connectivity for AI training and inference workloads.

## Why it matters

As AI clusters grow, network power and bandwidth are becoming constraints alongside GPUs; programmable optical switching is one route to improving utilisation without simply adding more electrical infrastructure.
