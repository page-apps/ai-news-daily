## Summary

Traversal announced general availability of Incident Workers, a public beta of Alert Workers and a Workers console for deploying and managing the fleet. Incident Workers investigate incidents end to end in Slack, Teams or the web console, while Alert Workers reason across signals, deduplicate noise and attach explanations to alerts.

## Why it matters

The launch moves agentic SRE from chat-assisted diagnosis towards persistent workers with an operational control surface. Teams will need clear evidence and rollback boundaries before delegating production investigations and alert triage.
