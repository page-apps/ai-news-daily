## Summary

AIR emerged from stealth with $50 million raised across two seed rounds and a platform that discovers and continuously vets agent skills, tools and other components. The company says its system can block unsafe external or software interactions and provide a marketplace of reviewed extensions.

## Why it matters

Agent extensions are becoming executable supply-chain inputs, but they are often installed from sources outside normal package review. A dedicated vetting layer shows the security market is beginning to treat agent skills and add-ons as production dependencies.
