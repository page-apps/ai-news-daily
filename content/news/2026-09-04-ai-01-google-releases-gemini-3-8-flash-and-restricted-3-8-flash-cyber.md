## Summary

Google says Gemini 3.8 Flash improves reasoning, coding and agentic tasks over 3.7 while keeping its introductory API price at $0.75 per million input tokens and $3.75 per million output tokens. Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber is offered through Fairwind to trusted defenders, with Google reporting strong vulnerability discovery and automated patching performance.

## Why it matters

A low-cost model moving towards frontier agent performance changes the economics of high-volume agents, while the Cyber variant shows providers are shipping dual-use capability with access controls. The benchmarks and security results remain vendor-reported.
