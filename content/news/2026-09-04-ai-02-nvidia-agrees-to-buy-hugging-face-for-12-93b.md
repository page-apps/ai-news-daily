## Summary

Nvidia said it will acquire Hugging Face for $12.93B, including a $1B employee retention plan. Hugging Face serves more than 18 million developers, researchers and creators, and Nvidia said it will remain an open platform supporting multicloud and multi-accelerator development.

## Why it matters

The agreement puts Nvidia's chip, model, data and application ecosystems under one ownership umbrella while preserving a major distribution channel for open models. It materially changes the competitive position of a platform previously treated as an independent counterweight to vertically integrated providers.
