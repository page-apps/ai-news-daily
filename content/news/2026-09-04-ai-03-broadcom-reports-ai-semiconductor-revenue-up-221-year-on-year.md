## Summary

Broadcom reported Q3 fiscal 2026 AI semiconductor revenue of $16.7B, up 221% year on year and 54% quarter on quarter. It guided to $21.7B of AI semiconductor revenue in Q4, while total Q3 revenue was $29.6B.

## Why it matters

This is hard operating evidence that custom accelerators and AI networking are becoming a major semiconductor growth engine, not just a venture narrative. The figures also show how quickly hyperscaler AI demand is concentrating purchasing power among a few infrastructure suppliers.
