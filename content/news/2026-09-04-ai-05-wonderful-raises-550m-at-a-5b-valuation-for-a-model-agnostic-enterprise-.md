## Summary

Wonderful raised $550M in a Series C led by Insight Partners, more than doubling its valuation to $5B in under six months. The company has shifted from customer-service agents towards a model-agnostic AI OS and operates across more than 35 countries.

## Why it matters

The round reflects capital moving towards integration and deployment layers that make multiple models usable inside real organisations. The valuation is a financing price, not proof of durable revenue or product-market fit, but the model-agnostic positioning shows enterprises are trying to keep model choice flexible.
