## Summary

Lyte announced a $165M Series C led by Maverick Silicon, taking its post-money valuation to $1.6B. Its LyteVision platform combines vision, imaging and inertial sensors with custom silicon to measure motion and deliver synchronised data to robotic systems.

## Why it matters

Robotics often fails at perception and calibration before it fails at high-level reasoning, so better sensing can be a foundational constraint on physical AI. The investment suggests capital is flowing into the full embodied stack, although real deployment scale is not yet disclosed.
