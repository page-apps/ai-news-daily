## Summary

Antioch raised a $32M Series A led by Greylock, with participation from A*, Category Ventures, Box Group and Icehouse Ventures. The company says its platform builds high-fidelity simulations for customer hardware and runs thousands of evaluations in parallel, with Amazon Ring as a disclosed customer and Nvidia and Nebius as partners.

## Why it matters

The financing highlights a strategic split in physical AI between collecting vast real-world datasets and generating calibrated synthetic data to cover rare or expensive scenarios. Simulation cannot replace field validation, but it could become the cheaper feedback loop that lets smaller robotics companies iterate without a billion-dollar data operation.
