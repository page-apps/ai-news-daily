## Summary

GitHub described shipped Copilot changes including compression of repetitive command output, removal of unused line-number prefixes, shorter task prompts and direct delivery of completed background results. It reported approximately 5% lower offline benchmark inference cost, 3% lower average daily model-inference cost per user and a 2.3% reduction in token-related usage, with no material quality regression in its tests.

## Why it matters

The work shows that coding-agent cost and latency can be improved through context and harness engineering, not only through new models. These optimisations can affect the economics of high-volume software development.
