GitHub made content exclusions generally available in the Copilot app and CLI. The clients respect enterprise, organisation and repository policies so excluded files are not supplied as agent context.

## Why it matters

The feature provides a direct control for keeping selected source material outside coding-agent context. It gives organisations a clearer policy boundary as Copilot expands beyond editor-only workflows.
