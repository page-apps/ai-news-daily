## Summary

pnpm 12.3 adds trust-policy flags to pnpm remove and pnpm update, verifying the lockfile against active policies. It also makes context-aware global commands such as node, deno, bun and pnpm-added shims native executables on every platform and improves local and tarball installs.

## Why it matters

Explicit policy verification gives teams a stronger dependency-supply-chain control during routine package operations. Native cross-platform shims should also make managed developer environments more predictable.
