## Summary

SCMP reported that Moody’s Ratings sees US hyperscalers spending far more on AI infrastructure, but a smaller gap in physical compute because Chinese buildouts are cheaper and benefit from policy incentives and lower-cost green power. The report projects Chinese technology companies’ capital expenditure will rise from about US$65 billion in 2025 to roughly US$140 billion in 2026 and US$165 billion in 2027.

## Why it matters

The competition may turn less on absolute spending than on how efficiently power, land and capital are converted into usable compute.
