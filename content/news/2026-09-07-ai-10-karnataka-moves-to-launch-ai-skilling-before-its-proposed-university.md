## Summary

Karnataka’s AI University consultation committee held its first meeting and agreed that certificate, diploma, executive-education and reskilling programmes can begin before the university is formally established. The proposed curriculum spans generative and agentic AI, cloud, cybersecurity, data centres, physical AI and semiconductors.

## Why it matters

The move reflects a wider shift from announcing future AI institutions to treating workforce capacity as immediate infrastructure.
