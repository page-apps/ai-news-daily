## Summary

Midscene 1.12.4 adds scoped AI contexts and stateless Test Runner workflows for browser and cross-platform UI automation. The release also fixes delayed Linux input, cancellation handling, missing-coordinate retries and duplicate YAML assertion errors.

## Why it matters

More isolated and repeatable AI-driven tests can make agent-based browser testing easier to integrate into CI and less dependent on persistent session state.
