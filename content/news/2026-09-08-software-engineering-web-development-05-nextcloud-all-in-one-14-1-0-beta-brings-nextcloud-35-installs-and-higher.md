## Summary

Nextcloud All-in-One 14.1.0 beta includes Nextcloud 34.0.3 while allowing new instances to install Nextcloud 35 directly. It adds adjustable logging for Caddy instances, raises the default upload limit to 1 GB and documents mounting multiple host directories.

## Why it matters

The release affects how self-hosted teams provision and operate a widely used web collaboration stack, particularly for larger uploads and multi-directory deployments.
