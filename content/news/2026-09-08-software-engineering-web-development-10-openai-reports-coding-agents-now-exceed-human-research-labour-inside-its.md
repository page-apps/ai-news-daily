## Summary

OpenAI says its research organisation uses the equivalent of 3.1 agent-workdays for every human workday, with the median high-usage researcher spending more than $600 per day at API prices. The company also reports more code, more experiments and increasing delegation of higher-level tasks, while noting that human researchers still set priorities, judge results and intervene on complex work.

## Why it matters

The disclosure is unusually concrete evidence about how coding agents are being deployed inside a frontier research organisation, while also showing that supervision and evaluation remain bottlenecks.
