## Summary

The all-cash transaction will add Alif's AI-native microcontrollers and fusion processors to Analog Devices' sensing, signal-processing and connectivity portfolio. The companies positioned the combination for low-latency, power-constrained systems spanning robotics, industrial automation, defence and digital health.

## Why it matters

Physical AI depends on tightly integrated sensing and local inference, not just larger cloud models. The acquisition is a significant signal that semiconductor companies are consolidating the edge-compute stack needed for real-world intelligent systems.
