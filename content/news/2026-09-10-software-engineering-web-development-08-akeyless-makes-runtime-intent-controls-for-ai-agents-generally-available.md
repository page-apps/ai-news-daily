## Summary

Agentic Runtime Authority adds real-time intent-based access control on top of Akeyless’s credential-brokering layer. General availability includes integrations for Claude Enterprise, OpenAI Codex and Amazon Bedrock AgentCore, plus session termination, investigation views and event forwarding to security tools.

## Why it matters

It represents a shift from granting agents access to governing each action they attempt after access has been granted.
