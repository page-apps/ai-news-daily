The amended law applies espionage provisions to all foreign countries rather than only specific adversaries. It covers technologies including semiconductors, displays, batteries and artificial intelligence, with prison terms of at least three years for violations.

## Why it matters

The change raises the legal and security stakes for AI and semiconductor research, industrial partnerships and technology transfer in South Korea.
