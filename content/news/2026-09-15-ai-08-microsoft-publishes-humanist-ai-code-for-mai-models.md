## Summary

Microsoft's new code says people should retain meaningful control over MAI systems, including the ability to interrupt, correct or shut them down. It also rules out models expanding their own scope, pursuing unassigned goals or concealing relevant reasoning, while acknowledging that these constraints may reduce capability.

## Why it matters

The policy is a concrete attempt to turn shutdown cooperation and bounded autonomy into explicit model-development requirements.
