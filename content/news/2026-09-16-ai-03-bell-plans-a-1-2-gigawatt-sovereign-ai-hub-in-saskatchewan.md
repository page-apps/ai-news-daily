## Summary

Bell and the Government of Saskatchewan announced a non-binding agreement to add up to 900 megawatts of capacity to Bell AI Fabric, creating a path to a 1.2-gigawatt AI infrastructure hub. The project is expected to involve more than $50 billion in capital investment and a new Bell AI Fabric headquarters in Saskatchewan.

## Why it matters

The proposal shows sovereign AI infrastructure becoming a national-scale power, data-residency and industrial-policy project rather than only a cloud buildout.
