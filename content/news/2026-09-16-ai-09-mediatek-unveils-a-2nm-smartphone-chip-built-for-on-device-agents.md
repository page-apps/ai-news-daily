## Summary

MediaTek announced the Dimensity 9600 Pro, a 2nm smartphone SoC with dual-NPU architecture and support for on-device applications using models up to 30 billion parameters. MediaTek claims 55% higher token generation per watt and a 40% reduction in always-on AI power consumption compared with the prior generation.

## Why it matters

The chip pushes agentic AI further into mass-market mobile hardware, where power efficiency and local privacy are as important as raw model capability.
