## Summary

The Spanish Data Protection Agency said it received the first reported notification of a personal-data breach allegedly carried out by an AI agent using a widely known large language model. The agency said the case remains under review and that neither the model nor its provider infrastructure was compromised.

## Why it matters

The report is an early regulatory signal that agent-enabled intrusion is entering real incident response, even though one case cannot establish a broader trend.
