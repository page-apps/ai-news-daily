## Summary

Cohere and Aleph Alpha announced a definitive business-combination agreement on 16 September, following an earlier plan to combine. The proposed company would be headquartered across Toronto and Berlin, retain Aleph Alpha's Heidelberg research centre and target sovereign deployments with partners including Germany's STACKIT.

## Why it matters

The deal would consolidate North American and European sovereign-AI capabilities into a larger alternative to US- and China-centred infrastructure, although execution and regulatory approval remain uncertain.
