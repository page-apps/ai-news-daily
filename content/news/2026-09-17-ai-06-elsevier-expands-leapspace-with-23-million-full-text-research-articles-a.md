## Summary

Elsevier announced the expanded LeapSpace corpus and an independent advisory board on 16 September. The company says the full-text content covers 56% of recent global research citations and 42% of articles published since 2021, while the workspace supports literature reviews, study protocols, data, manuscripts and theses.

## Why it matters

Scientific AI depends on trusted, licensed content and provenance as much as on model quality; Elsevier is packaging both corpus scale and governance, though its coverage figures are company analysis.
