## Summary

RWS announced the public preview of its Tridion agentic platform on 16 September for medtech, pharmaceutical and manufacturing documentation workflows. The platform connects external changes to governed content, maintains audit trails and keeps publication decisions with people; RWS says early users cut impact-assessment time from days to hours.

## Why it matters

This is a practical model for deploying agents in regulated environments, where evidence, version control and human approval are part of the workflow rather than post-launch safeguards.
