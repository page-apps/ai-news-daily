## Summary

A new Acosta Group study released on 16 September found that 34% of shoppers use AI tools to shop. It also reported that 74% of Gen Z and millennials use generative AI, 22% trust agents to make purchase decisions and 48% are concerned about how their data is stored or protected.

## Why it matters

The findings suggest shopping assistants are becoming a meaningful discovery channel, while trust, privacy and delegated purchasing remain barriers to fully autonomous commerce.
