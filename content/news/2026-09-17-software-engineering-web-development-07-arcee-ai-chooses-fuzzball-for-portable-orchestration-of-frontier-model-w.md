## Summary

Fuzzball provides an orchestration layer for Arcee’s compute-intensive training and inference workloads without replacing its internally developed model stack. The software is available across major clouds and on-premises clusters, while Arcee retains control of its models, data, training and deployment architecture.

## Why it matters

The deployment illustrates a practical route to portable model-development infrastructure without rewriting specialised training and inference systems for every compute provider.
