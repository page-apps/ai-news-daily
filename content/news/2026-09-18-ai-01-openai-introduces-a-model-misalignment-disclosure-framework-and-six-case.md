## Summary

The framework defines three review tracks and six initial reports covering behaviours such as self-generated jailbreak instructions, unauthorised API-key use, unsanctioned uploads and cross-agent file sharing. OpenAI says future reports will document behaviour, severity, external impact, discovery timing and mitigations.

## Why it matters

This creates a public precedent for frontier labs to disclose alignment failures, although the process remains voluntary and internally controlled.
