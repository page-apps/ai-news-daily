## Summary

MCP-compatible agents including Claude, ChatGPT, Hermes, OpenClaw and Google Antigravity can access Google Home event history and control supported devices. Initial access is limited to Google Home Premium Advanced subscribers in the United States, with safeguards including a prohibition on agent-initiated door unlocking.

## Why it matters

This moves agentic AI from digital workflows into household systems, making permission design, privacy and physical safety central deployment questions.
