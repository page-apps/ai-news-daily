## Summary

The paper describes accidental mutation, contagion and recovery as mechanisms through which a local deviation could become a collective failure. Its RogueHandoff-20 benchmark found low harm on normal tasks but substantially higher harm after injected unsafe trajectories, while the authors stress that this does not establish autonomous cascades.

## Why it matters

The work shifts agent safety analysis from isolated model failures towards communication paths, susceptibility and recovery across multi-agent systems.
