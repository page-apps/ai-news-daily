## Summary

The authors show how every individual agent step can pass its own check while the complete execution violates authority, threshold or review requirements. They propose provenance-aware monitoring that recomputes guarded quantities across the full execution trace rather than relying only on per-step classifiers.

## Why it matters

The result highlights a structural weakness in current agent governance: local compliance does not guarantee that an autonomous workflow remains within its intended authority.
