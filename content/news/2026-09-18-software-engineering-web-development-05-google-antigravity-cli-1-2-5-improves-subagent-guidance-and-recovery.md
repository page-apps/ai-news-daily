## Summary

Antigravity CLI 1.2.5 automatically gives Markdown-defined agents that declare subagent invocation access to the available subagent roster and delegation instructions. It also preserves background task names, clears definitively rejected credentials, records externally interrupted commands as cancelled and fixes frozen thinking frames.

## Why it matters

The changes improve the operational clarity of multi-agent sessions, particularly when agents run asynchronously or are controlled remotely.
