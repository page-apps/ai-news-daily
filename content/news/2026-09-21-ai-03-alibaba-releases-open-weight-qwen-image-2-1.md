## Summary

Qwen Image 2.1 is available through Hugging Face and ModelScope for visual generation and editing tasks. The model supports transparent-image generation and can use up to ten reference images, under a research and evaluation licence.

## Why it matters

A relatively compact open-weight image model broadens local and self-hosted access to higher-end visual generation workflows. Its licence limits commercial use, but the release still increases competitive pressure on proprietary image platforms.
