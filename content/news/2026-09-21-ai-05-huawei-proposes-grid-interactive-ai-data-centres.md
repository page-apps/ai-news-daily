## Summary

Huawei’s grid-interactive AIDC 1.0 combines grid-friendly UPS systems, intelligent batteries, grid-forming storage and AI-powered liquid cooling. The company is positioning the design around tokens per watt and tighter coordination between AI workloads and electricity infrastructure.

## Why it matters

AI data centres are becoming large, volatile electricity loads, making power quality and grid flexibility as important as accelerator supply. Architectures that coordinate compute with storage and cooling could affect where future AI campuses can be built and operated.
