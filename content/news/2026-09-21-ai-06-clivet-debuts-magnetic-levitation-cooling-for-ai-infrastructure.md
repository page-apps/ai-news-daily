## Summary

The company announced a dual-rotor magnetic-levitation compressor alongside cooling distribution equipment and a joint power-and-cooling coordination solution with CLOU. The products were presented as responses to rising rack density, thermal-management and efficiency requirements in AI facilities.

## Why it matters

Cooling is becoming a binding constraint as AI accelerator power density rises. New compressor, liquid-cooling and power-coordination designs could expand the usable density of AI clusters, although the announcement provides no independent performance data.
