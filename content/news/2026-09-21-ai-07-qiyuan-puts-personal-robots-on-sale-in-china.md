## Summary

Qiyuan announced prices starting at 19,999 yuan for Q1 and 29,999 yuan for the T1 Pro, with deliveries scheduled from October 1. The company described the robots as foldable or transformable personal platforms and said it would provide SDK and hardware-development access.

## Why it matters

The launch moves personal robots from demonstrations toward an identifiable consumer product category with published prices and delivery dates. Actual usefulness, reliability and support remain unproven, but developer access could help create an ecosystem around early hardware.
