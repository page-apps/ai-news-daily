## Summary

Dongfeng’s chief engineer said Xiaodong would begin factory work in October and move to small-batch, limited-site trials by year-end. The automaker is targeting human-level capability by the end of 2027, which is a roadmap claim rather than demonstrated performance.

## Why it matters

Automotive factories are a natural proving ground for embodied AI because they offer structured environments, repeatable tasks and existing automation infrastructure. Dongfeng’s timeline is another indication that Chinese automakers are treating humanoid robots as an extension of industrial production rather than a separate consumer experiment.
