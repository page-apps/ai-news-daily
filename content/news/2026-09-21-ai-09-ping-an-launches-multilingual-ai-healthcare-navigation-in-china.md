## Summary

GlobalCare supports real-time two-way speech and text translation across 19 languages, including medical terminology. Ping An Good Doctor said the service was available for trial in Shenzhen after its unveiling at the Shenzhen Sci-Tech Innovation Exhibition.

## Why it matters

This is a concrete healthcare deployment in which language models support access and navigation rather than merely summarising medical information. Its trial status means clinical safety and effectiveness are still open questions, but the model could reduce access barriers for international patients.
