## Summary

The company said the launch covered the Futurist, Master Mini and three FX Aegis configurations, alongside solutions for education, research, security and inspection. Faraday Future also said the Futurist was on sale and that the Master Mini would start at $9,990; independent evidence of deployment or capability was not provided.

## Why it matters

The launch illustrates an attempt to commercialise embodied AI as a multi-form product ecosystem rather than a single general-purpose robot. Its significance depends on whether Faraday Future can convert a broad catalogue and low entry price into reliable real-world deployments.
