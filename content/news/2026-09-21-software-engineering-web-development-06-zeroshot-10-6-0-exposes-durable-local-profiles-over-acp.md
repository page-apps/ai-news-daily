## Summary

Zeroshot 10.6.0 exposes local profiles as ACP agents over stdio. A single ACP session keeps its workspace and provider sessions alive while each prompt remains a separate durable run with fresh Git provenance and observable active turns.

## Why it matters

The change makes Zeroshot easier to embed in agent-aware editors and orchestrators without sacrificing run-level provenance or session observability.
