## Summary

mise 2026.9.12 lets tasks declare required daemons and lets projects select daemon subsets through daemon groups. Automatic ports, stable per-worktree hostnames and database, NATS and SpiceDB presets allow parallel checkouts to run the same development stack with less manual configuration.

## Why it matters

Teams running several branches or coding-agent worktrees can provision repeatable local services without hand-assigned ports or ad hoc process scripts.
