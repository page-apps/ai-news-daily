## Summary

Falcon Router will initially process email and web intake and route prioritised information to Veeva Safety, Veeva QMS, Veeva MedInquiry or designated inboxes. Early-adopter availability is planned for January 2027, so the announcement describes a forthcoming deployment rather than an already broad production rollout.

## Why it matters

Regulated industries are beginning to assign agents to high-volume safety workflows, where traceability, correct routing and human review matter more than general-purpose conversational ability.
