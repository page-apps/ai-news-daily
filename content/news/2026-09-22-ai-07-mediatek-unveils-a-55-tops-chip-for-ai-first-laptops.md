## Summary

The 3-nanometre SoC combines an eight-core CPU, an 11-core GPU and an NPU 890 rated at up to 55 TOPS. MediaTek said the chip will power an upcoming premium Googlebook device and support Gemini Intelligence features locally.

## Why it matters

The launch extends the shift toward dedicated AI acceleration in mainstream personal computers, where local inference can improve latency, privacy and resilience while reducing dependence on cloud calls.
