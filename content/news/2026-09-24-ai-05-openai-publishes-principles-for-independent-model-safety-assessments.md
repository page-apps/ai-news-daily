## Summary

The proposed assessments cover safety cases, safeguards, capability thresholds and misalignment incidents. OpenAI says evaluators should have access proportionate to their claims, disclose methods and retain editorial independence, while the company may request sensitive redactions.

## Why it matters

This is a concrete governance mechanism for moving frontier-model oversight beyond internal testing, although partners and access terms remain unresolved.
