## Summary

Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS targets expressive, controllable voice creation, while Flash-Lite TTS is optimised for high-volume generation and voice agents. Google says both models include consent verification, SynthID watermarking and C2PA credentials for replicated voices.

## Why it matters

Voice generation is moving from fixed presets toward programmable, multilingual performance and identity creation, increasing both creative utility and impersonation risk.
