## Summary

The Change Request Agent identifies and summarises changes across applications, while another agent supports submission and underwriting preparation. Vertafore says early testing showed up to 70% less review time and up to 98% fewer missed changes, with general availability expected in autumn.

The deployment illustrates how vertical agents are being embedded into regulated operational systems, where human approval and traceability remain part of the workflow.
