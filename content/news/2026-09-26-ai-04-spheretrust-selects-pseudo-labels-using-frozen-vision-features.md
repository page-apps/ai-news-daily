## Summary

SphereTrust ranks candidate masks using the geometry of frozen self-supervised features rather than an additional quality model. Across eight candidate pools, it exceeded the strongest evaluated external baseline on six pools by 1.7 to 9.3 Dice points.

## Why it matters

More reliable pseudo-label selection can reduce annotation costs and improve the quality of training data for vision systems.
