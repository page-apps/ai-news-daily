## Summary

PoEM models post-trained policies as combinations in log-policy space and estimates new policy outcomes from existing policy outputs. Experiments across synthetic and real rewards cover both text and image modalities, potentially reducing repeated reinforcement-learning runs.

## Why it matters

If the method generalises, it could lower the compute and iteration cost of post-training frontier and specialised models.
