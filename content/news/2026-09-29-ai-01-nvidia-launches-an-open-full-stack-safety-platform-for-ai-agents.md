The platform combines OpenShell, which enforces policies around agent execution, with Sentry, a BlueField-4 DPU watchdog that can quarantine agents outside their boundaries. NVIDIA says more than 100 organisations are collaborating on the platform, although the announcement presents a reference design rather than independent deployment results.
