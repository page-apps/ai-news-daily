## Summary

The initial system combines TerraFlow's long-duration uninterruptible power technology, DG Matrix's programmable transformer platform and Dell PowerEdge servers. The companies said the architecture is intended to buffer rapid compute-load changes between the grid and the data centre.

## Why it matters

AI data centres are creating power loads that vary more rapidly than conventional grid infrastructure was designed to handle, making behind-the-meter flexibility strategically important.
