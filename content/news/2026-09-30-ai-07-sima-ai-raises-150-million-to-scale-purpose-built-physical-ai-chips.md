## Summary

The round brings SiMa.ai’s total funding to $500 million and will fund its Palette Neat agentic environment and next-generation hardware. The company is targeting 1,000 dense TOPS for chips aimed at humanoid robots, drones, vehicles and industrial systems.

## Why it matters

The financing strengthens the push toward specialised, power-efficient inference at the edge, where robotics and autonomous systems cannot rely solely on cloud GPUs.
