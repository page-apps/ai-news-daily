The agreements cover compute for Meta’s AI research, model development and training using NVIDIA’s DSX platform. They extend an existing relationship in Australia, where Meta is already using GB300 NVL72 systems at Firmus’s Melbourne facility.

## Why it matters

The deal illustrates how frontier-lab capacity is spreading into regional AI factories that integrate GPUs, networking, cooling and power rather than operating as conventional data-centre leases.
