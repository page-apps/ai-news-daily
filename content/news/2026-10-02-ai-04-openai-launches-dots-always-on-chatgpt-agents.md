## Summary

Dots run on GPT-6 Astra and can connect to more than 4,000 applications, with initial access through paid ChatGPT tiers and selected enterprise plans. OpenAI is also integrating the agents with Slack, Teams and Microsoft's Agent 365 governance stack.

## Why it matters

The launch moves consumer-facing AI agents from conversational assistance toward persistent, delegated work with access to external systems.
