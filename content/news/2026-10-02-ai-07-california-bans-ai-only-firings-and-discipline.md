## Summary

The law bars employers from relying solely on automated decision systems for firing or disciplining workers and requires notice about AI involvement and relevant data. It is scheduled to take effect on July 1, 2027.

## Why it matters

The measure establishes one of the clearest US limits on high-impact employment decisions made by AI and may become a template for other states.
