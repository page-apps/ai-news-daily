## Summary

PDF artifacts now render as real pages with scrolling, page indicators and zoom controls, while extracted text remains available for copying and screen readers. The release also validates saved PDFs and falls back to text cards for password-protected, oversized or bomb-like files.

## Why it matters

Agent workflows that produce reports or documents can now present and inspect PDFs in a safer, more usable way without requiring agents to retype document contents.
