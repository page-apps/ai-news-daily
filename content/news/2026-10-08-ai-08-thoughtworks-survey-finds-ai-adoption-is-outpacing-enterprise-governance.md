## Summary

Eighty-eight percent of surveyed CIOs said AI adoption is moving faster than governance, while 89 percent said their role increasingly includes redesigning workforce workflows. The survey also found that responsibility for AI budgets and workforce impacts is fragmented across organisations.

## Why it matters

The findings point to a durable organisational problem: accountability for AI is expanding faster than authority, controls and decision rights.
