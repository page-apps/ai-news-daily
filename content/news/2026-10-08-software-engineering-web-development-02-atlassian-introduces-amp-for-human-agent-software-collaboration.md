## Summary

Atlassian announced AMP alongside a new MCP server, richer Teamwork Graph context and agent-session visibility. The system connects local and cloud agent work with Jira, Confluence, Loom and Bitbucket while recording ownership and actions.

## Why it matters

It treats agents as auditable participants in software teams rather than isolated assistants operating outside normal collaboration systems.
