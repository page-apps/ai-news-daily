## Summary

The Standards for Publisher Usage Rights coalition now has more than 80 members across more than 20 countries. The expansion comes as publishers pursue licensing standards and litigation against AI companies over training and commercial reuse of their material.

## Why it matters

A broader rights coalition increases pressure on model providers to negotiate licensing, provenance and usage terms rather than relying on informal access to publisher content.
