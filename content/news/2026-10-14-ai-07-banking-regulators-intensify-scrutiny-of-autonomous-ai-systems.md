## Summary

Reuters reported that the Federal Reserve, FDIC and OCC are asking banks how agents are monitored, approved, audited and shut down. Regulators are also examining risks from foreign-developed models, cyberattacks and agents exceeding their authority.

## Why it matters

The inquiries indicate that agent governance is becoming a supervisory expectation in critical financial infrastructure, not merely an internal risk-management choice.
