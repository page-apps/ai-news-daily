## Summary

Gufo 0.24.0 adds image-input support for embeddings and allows repeated served-model names for a single text model. The release also improves sampler-field compatibility and connection accounting, and adds EmbeddingGemma 2 attention support on matrix cores.

## Why it matters

Multimodal embedding inputs and more flexible model naming make local or self-hosted inference services easier to integrate into retrieval and application pipelines.
