## Summary

FireMon’s agents can search policy data, discover traffic paths, construct queries and prepare Policy Plan changes without automatically deploying them. The platform also exposes selected policy data and change-preparation operations to external workflows through an MCP server, with scoped permissions and audit trails.

## Why it matters

It brings agent-assisted investigation and change preparation into application and network-security operations while retaining human approval for consequential changes.
