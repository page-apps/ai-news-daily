## Summary

Rime says Prism responds in roughly 300 milliseconds, preserves acoustic cues such as hesitation and frustration, and generates text before speech so organisations can review or modify responses. The model is designed for private or on-premises deployment and supports fine-tuning on customer conversations.

## Why it matters

Prism addresses a key trade-off in voice agents: natural audio interaction without giving up auditability, policy enforcement or deployment control.
